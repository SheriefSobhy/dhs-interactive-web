Flooth · Data choices

Data deletion

Flooth Local V1

This page explains how to request help with data deletion and what you can remove directly from your device.

Request help by email

To ask DHS Interactive a question or make a request about Flooth-related data, email dhs.interactive@gmail.com. Please describe your request without including financial records, passwords, or other sensitive information. Flooth Local V1 does not create a Flooth account or provide an in-app account deletion flow.

Because Local Mode financial records are stored on your device and are not sent to a Flooth cloud service, DHS Interactive cannot remove those records from your device remotely. Use the controls below to remove local data.

Remove local financial data

Your financial records are held in Flooth’s app-private database on your device. Delete records using available in-app features, or remove/reset Flooth’s app data through your Android device settings or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling can permanently remove the on-device database. If you want to keep a copy, export an encrypted backup first and store it separately.

Encrypted backup files you exported are saved to a location selected through Android’s document picker. Flooth does not upload those files to a Flooth server. To remove an exported copy, delete it through the storage or file-provider service where you saved it. Other providers may retain or synchronize copies under their own terms.

Firebase Analytics

Turning Usage Analytics off in More → Privacy tells Flooth to disable future Analytics collection after the app applies that choice. It does not delete information already collected by Firebase. Firebase Analytics retention and deletion are subject to Firebase service controls and the property settings; DHS Interactive has not verified the current Analytics retention settings for this release. We cannot promise a particular deletion time or that an email request will remove data from Firebase.

Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics operates independently from the Usage Analytics choice. Turning Analytics off does not turn Crashlytics off, delete existing crash reports, or change Crashlytics collection. Firebase describes a 90-day period before it starts removing certain Crashlytics data from live and backup systems, but DHS Interactive has not verified project-specific settings that may affect this service. That information is not a guarantee of when a particular report or identifier will be deleted. We cannot promise that DHS Interactive can directly delete Crashlytics data on request.

Firebase diagnostic and Analytics data are subject to Google’s/Firebase’s service retention and deletion controls. See Firebase’s privacy and retention information for service-level details.

More information

For the full description of Flooth’s storage, services, retention, and choices, read the Flooth Privacy Policy. For other help, visit Flooth support.
